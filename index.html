YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy overview

Linkup uses account information and content you choose to submit to operate the service. Do not publish sensitive personal information.

Before public launch, the operator must publish a complete privacy notice explaining data collection, retention, deletion requests, contact details and any third-party services used.

COMMUNITY EXPECTATIONS

Terms & community rules

  • Be respectful. Harassment, threats and hateful content are not allowed.
  • Do not post private information about another person.
  • Do not impersonate others, spam, or upload content you do not have permission to share.
  • Use reporting and blocking tools when needed.
  • Linkup may remove content or restrict accounts that break the rules.

This starter policy is not a substitute for legal review before a public launch.
